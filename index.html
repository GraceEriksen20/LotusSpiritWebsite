Welcome to Lotus Spirit

Lotus Spirit Studio is a place for intentional movement, self-reflection, and restoration. Through yoga, guided wellness experiences, and healing practices, we create space for people to slow down, reconnect, and feel supported in their journey.

Our studio is rooted in the belief that growth can be both challenging and beautiful, and that healing begins when we give ourselves room to breathe.
